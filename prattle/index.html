Prattle

Dictation that stays on your iPhone

Prattle turns what you say into clean, punctuated text, in the app or in any app with the Prattle keyboard. It works on your iPhone with no account and no subscription, or with your own API key for a cloud model.

Need help? Email prattle@paulbendorsamuel.com, or tap Settings › About › Send feedback in the app.

Getting started

  1. Open Prattle and allow microphone access when asked.
  2. Tap the mic button and start talking. Your words appear as you speak.
  3. Tap Done. The transcript is saved to your history and copied, ready to paste.

Tap any transcript to play the recording, compare the cleaned text with the raw transcript, share it, or run it again with another engine.

The Prattle keyboard

Set it up

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app and go to General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard, then choose Prattle.
  2. Tap Prattle in the list and turn on Allow Full Access. Dictation needs it; typing works without it.

Prattle's own Settings has an Open Settings button that takes you straight there.

Dictate in any app

  1. In any text field, hold or tap the globe key to switch to the Prattle keyboard, then tap Dictate.
  2. The first time, Prattle opens to start the microphone. Tap the app name at the top left of the screen (for example ◀ Messages) to go back, and keep talking.
  3. Tap Done on the keyboard. Your words are typed where the cursor is.

For a few minutes after each dictation, the next one starts straight away without leaving your app. You can change how long in Prattle under Settings › Keyboard › Keep mic ready.

Other keys

  • Pause and Resume stop and restart a dictation without ending it.
  • Insert types your latest transcript again, and copies it.
  • Hold the space bar and slide to move the cursor.

Engines and API keys

On this iPhone (free). Parakeet v2 comes with Prattle and is the default. It runs entirely on your iPhone, works offline and handles English. Parakeet v3 adds 25 European languages; tap Get in Settings to download it (about 640 MB).

In the cloud, with your own key. Add an API key from OpenAI, Groq, Google or OpenRouter in Settings, or add your own server under Add custom model. Your audio goes straight from your iPhone to that provider, and you pay them directly. Prattle never sees your audio or your key.

Get a key from OpenAI, Groq, Google AI Studio or OpenRouter. Keys are stored in your iPhone’s Keychain.

Questions

The keyboard says to turn on Full Access

Dictation needs Full Access so the keyboard can talk to the Prattle app. Go to Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Prattle and turn on Allow Full Access.

Why does Prattle open when I tap Dictate?

iOS doesn’t let keyboards use the microphone, so the keyboard asks the Prattle app to record. Tap the app name at the top left to go back; recording carries on in the background.

Why is the orange microphone dot still showing?

After a dictation, Prattle keeps the microphone ready for a few minutes so the next one starts instantly. Nothing is recorded until you tap Dictate. To shorten or turn this off, go to Settings › Keyboard › Keep mic ready in Prattle.

My recording paused on its own

Recordings pause after a stretch of silence, in case one is left running by accident. Tap Resume to carry on. You can change the time under Settings › Recording › Pause after silence.

Where did my old transcripts go?

Prattle deletes transcripts and recordings automatically after the time set under Settings › History, 24 hours by default. Choose a longer period, or keep them forever.

Is my audio private?

With Parakeet, your audio never leaves your iPhone. With a cloud engine, it goes only to the provider you chose. Prattle has no servers and no account. See the privacy policy.

The punctuation cleanup isn’t working

Cleanup uses Apple Intelligence, so it needs an iPhone that supports it, with Apple Intelligence turned on. Check Settings › Cleanup › Fix punctuation in Prattle.
