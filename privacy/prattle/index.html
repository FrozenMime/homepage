Prattle Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Prattle has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your recordings and transcripts stay on your iPhone, and your audio only goes to a transcription provider if you choose one.

Who makes Prattle

Prattle is made by Paul Bendor-Samuel. This policy covers the Prattle iPhone app and its keyboard.

Your recordings and transcripts

Recordings and transcripts are stored only on your iPhone. You can delete them at any time, and Prattle deletes them automatically after the period you choose in Settings.

Transcription

With an on-device engine (Parakeet), your audio never leaves your iPhone.

If you choose a cloud engine, your audio and any custom words go directly from your iPhone to that provider (OpenAI, Groq, Google, OpenRouter, or a server you add yourself), using your own API key. They are handled under that provider’s privacy policy. Prattle never sees them.

API keys

API keys are stored in your iPhone’s Keychain and sent only to the provider they belong to.

The Prattle keyboard

The keyboard sends nothing over the network. What you type with it is not stored or shared.

Beta versions

TestFlight builds send anonymous usage events to PostHog, in the United States, so we can find and fix problems: for example, which engine was used, how long a transcription took, or whether it failed.

These events are tied to a random ID created on your device, not to your name, Apple ID or phone number. They never include your audio, transcripts, API keys, custom words or anything you type. App Store versions send nothing.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
